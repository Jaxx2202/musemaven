EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Problem
Muse Maven already has a before/after photo gallery live on musemavenatl.com, at least 10 pairs, but it sits on one subpage with no promotion, no video, and no connection to social or Google Business Profile. The content exists; the distribution doesn't.
Solution
Extend the existing capture habit to include short video, then build the missing piece: a consistent publishing pipeline that gets this content off that one page and into Instagram, GBP, and the review-to-content flow.
Why It Works
This isn't starting from zero. The hardest part of a content system, getting the team to actually capture results, is already a habit. The gap is video and distribution, both solvable without asking for new behavior at the point of service.
Timeline
Begins immediately since capture is already happening, video and a publishing calendar can layer in within the first two weeks. Compounds continuously from there, no fixed end date.
Closes
Reviews & Trust Video
HOW IT WORKS

Three stages, and only one of them is actually new.

Capture is already happening. The work is adding video to it and building the distribution that's never existed.

Stage 1
Expand

Add short video capture alongside the before/after photos already being taken. Same moment, same consent process, one more format.

Stage 2
Produce

Raw captures, new and the existing 10+ pairs, edited into short-form video and quote graphics on a fixed weekly cadence.

Stage 3
Distribute

Published across Instagram and Google Business Profile, not left on the standalone gallery page. The existing page stays, but stops being the only place this content lives.

1
Setup

Add video to the existing capture moment

The team is already capturing before/after photos with consent. This adds a short video clip to that same moment, no new habit required.

2
Setup

Consistent format for new and existing content

A shared format applied going forward, and retroactively to the existing 10+ pairs, so the whole library reads as one cohesive system instead of two eras of content.

3
Ongoing

Review-to-content pipeline

Existing written reviews from Google and elsewhere converted into quote graphics, giving Reviews & Trust a second life as visual content.

4
Ongoing

Weekly publishing cadence

A steady two-post-a-week rhythm, sustainable rather than a burst that trails off after the first month.

Why this closes two gaps at once

Reviews & Trust and Video were flagged as separate gaps, but they share the same root cause: proof exists but isn't circulating. One pipeline, run consistently, closes both.

Future extension: medical wellness

Muse Maven's site also lists GLP-1 weight management and peptide therapy, services outside this proposal's current scope. Once this pipeline is running, it's the natural place to eventually capture those results too, same system, no new campaign required. Noted here as a phase-2 extension, not part of this concept's current scope or price.
