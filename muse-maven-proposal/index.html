03 — THE COST OF INACTION

What these gaps are already costing, every week they stay open.

Quiet, ongoing cost

At a $300 average service ticket, even 2–3 missed inquiries a month from broken search visibility is $600–$900 in quiet monthly loss, before Word of Mouth or Content & SEO gaps are factored in. This isn't hypothetical, it's happening every week the metadata stays wrong.

04 — THE FINDABILITY FIX

Before the bigger campaigns, fix what's actively broken.

Not another gimmick or dream concept, a foundational correction. This is the one item in the proposal that isn't building something new, it's repairing what's already live and actively costing visibility.

Closes
AI Search & Google Search Content & SEO
  • 01
    Metadata Correction

    Rewrite title tags, meta descriptions, and alt text sitewide, starting with removing the mismatched piercing-shop copy currently live on the site.

  • 02
    AI / Answer-Engine Optimization

    Structure service pages so ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini can actually parse and recommend Muse Maven when someone asks for an esthetician in Atlanta.

  • 03
    Catalog-to-Content Pipeline

    Turn the existing service and product catalog into real, searchable pages, treatment guides, ingredient explainers, feeding both traditional SEO and AI search at once.

  • 04
    Google Business Profile (GBP) Cleanup

    GBP, sometimes called your Google Business Page or listing, is what controls what shows up when someone searches Muse Maven by name, hours, photos, reviews, directions. Correcting categories, consistent name/address/phone, photos, and a review-generation flow is often the single highest-leverage move for a local service business.

  • 05
    Directory & Citation Building

    Accurate listings on Yelp, local Atlanta beauty roundups, and esthetician associations, exactly the kind of grounding data AI search tools pull from.

Who does the work

LIVINGSTONdrive handles the backend execution, metadata, technical fixes, campaign builds, directly. Muse Maven's role is approvals, providing account access, and supplying content assets like photos, not day-to-day production.
